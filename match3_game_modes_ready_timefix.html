Specials

  • 4 in a row → Flame (💥) clears adjacent gems when matched
  • T / L → Star (✨) clears row + column when matched
  • 5 in a row → Hyper (🌈) swap to clear a color
  • 6+ in a row → Sun (☀️) clears row/column plus adjacent rows/columns when matched

Bombs (💣): Appear occasionally. Each move lowers their counter. If any reaches 0 before being cleared → game over.

Rocks (🪨): Appear occasionally after 2000 points. Cannot be matched but can be cleared by specials.

Locks (🔒): Appear occasionally after 3000 points. Cannot be swapped but can be cleared like normal.

Skulls (☠️): Begin appearing after 10000 points. A dangerous bomb that must be disarmed before the counter reaches 0. Cannot be swapped or matched. It must be destroyed by special gems.
